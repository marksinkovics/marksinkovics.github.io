Runlight shows your CI pipelines and merge requests at a glance, so you stop refreshing browser tabs to see whether your build passed or someone is waiting on your review. It works with GitLab.com and self-hosted GitLab.

It's a menu bar app on the Mac, and an app with widgets on iPhone and iPad.

## Features

- **Up Next:** one list of everything that needs you, in a sensible order, each with a reason: you broke the main branch, your merge request is ready to merge, a review has waited three days, your pipeline failed.
- **Pipelines:** the latest pipeline on each branch you care about, with status, duration and the failing job. Read the end of a failed job's log without leaving the app.
- **Merge requests:** waiting for your review, assigned to you, and your own, with what's still missing: approvals, open threads, a failing pipeline, conflicts.
- **Widgets:** the status light and Up Next on your Home Screen, or a small status on your Lock Screen.
- **Quiet by design:** snooze a merge request until it changes, ignore a pipeline until the next run, or mute a whole project.

## Private by design

Runlight only needs read access and never changes anything on GitLab. It talks to your GitLab directly: there is no Runlight server, and nothing is collected. Accounts, watched projects and snoozes sync between your devices through your own iCloud. See the [privacy policy](/projects/runlight/privacy-policy) for details.
