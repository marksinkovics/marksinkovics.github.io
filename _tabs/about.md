Hello 👋

I'm a software developer working on mobile apps and cross-platform C++. I started my career as an iOS developer writing Objective-C on iPhone OS 3.2, then worked on macOS apps in C/C++, Objective-C and Swift. Since then I've built apps for both iOS and Android, working in Swift, Objective-C, Kotlin, Java and TypeScript, and I write C++ that runs on macOS, Windows and Linux.

I consider myself a technology enthusiast who is keen on learning new technologies, techniques, design patterns and programming languages. Fortunately, my profession is my passion, and I love what I can achieve with it.

I spend some of my free time on personal projects, where I can dive deep into different and challenging problems. Right now that's [Runlight](/projects/runlight), a Mac, iPhone and iPad app for keeping an eye on GitLab pipelines and merge requests. Before it came [Sudoku](/projects/sudoku), a hobby operating system ([Feather OS](/projects/feather-os)) and a 3D game engine ([Atakama](/projects/atakama)). When I face an obstacle, I prefer to understand it and find the best solution for it: I learn the most when I can see the big picture.

My motto is "quality over quantity". I am obsessed with quality in my work. Occasionally, though, it has to be put aside to get things done, and improved later.
